REDISTRIBUTION LICENSE CLEARANCE REMEDIATION (R5):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In Release R5, all previously bundled GNU General Public License (GPL)
and copyleft components have been systematically eliminated from the
runtime bundle:

1. PyAV / FFmpeg / x264 / x265 Codecs: REMOVED.
   - Replaced with a standalone, version-pinned PCM-WAV-only audio decoder
     (`native/python/voice_runtime/wav_decoder.py`) accepting strictly
     controlled 16-bit 16kHz PCM WAV input.
   - `faster-whisper` is patched via an MIT-licensed patch
     (`patches/faster-whisper-1.2.1-wav-only.patch`, upstream commit
     65882eee9f5cdbeeb2d877f1131d48cf241b327d) removing PyAV import requirements.
   - Runtime artifact contains zero PyAV, FFmpeg, libx264, or libx265 binaries.

2. phonemizer / eSpeak-NG / espeakng-loader: REMOVED.
   - Replaced with Misaki English G2P (`misaki==0.9.4`, Apache-2.0),
     producing phonemes directly for Kokoro ONNX inference.
   - Neither phonemizer nor eSpeak-NG DLLs/data are packaged or loaded.
